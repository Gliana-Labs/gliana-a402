# Demo video — narration script

Spoken by `elevenlabs/eleven-v3` through the gateway itself: the voice is a
model this API sells. Every figure on screen is a real capture — the 402 body,
the settle receipt, and the on-chain transaction, including its zero fee.

**0.7s** — Gliana A.I. Pay per result, over x402 on Algorand.

**10.0s** — Every generative A.I. provider sells by the month, to people with accounts. That suits a daily user. It fits no software agent at all, because an agent cannot fill in a signup form or hold a card on file.

**27.68s** — So we removed the account. You ask for one model run, with no key and no signup, and the answer is a 402 that names its own price.

**42.88s** — The challenge is the price list. The exact amount, the asset, and the address it goes to, all quoted before anything runs. This one costs a tenth of a cent in U.S.D.C.

**58.4s** — It is gasless on purpose. The facilitator's fee payer funds the group, so the buyer holds U.S.D.C. and needs no ALGO at all. That is what lets an agent carry a single asset and still transact.

**77.68s** — Paying is one command. The client signs an asset transfer group and retries the request.

**87.76s** — Settlement confirms first, and only then is the output released. A settle failure never gives the result away for free.

**99.2s** — Here it is on MainNet. A real U.S.D.C. transfer to the payTo address, and look at the fee: zero, paid by the facilitator.

**112.56s** — The money moved by exactly the quoted amount. One thousand micro U.S.D.C. In, one tenth of a cent.

**124.8s** — We build the payment requirements, never the buyer. The facilitator decodes the signed group against our amount and our payTo, so a buyer cannot quote themselves a cheaper price or redirect the payment.

**140.96s** — And we validate before charging. A request that cannot succeed is refused at the 402, not charged and then failed.

**153.36s** — Forty-seven resources share one payTo. Image, video, music, speech, chat, and fifty utility tools, every one priced per call on Algorand MainNet.

**168.4s** — It is live now. The code is public, and so is every endpoint.
